### Bull case
- **Proprietary, industrial-scale biology dataset + automated labs** — a real data/infrastructure asset that's hard to replicate, and the engine behind partnerships with Roche/Genentech and others.
- Improved cash discipline (burn guided <$390M, runway into early 2028); narrower losses.

### Bear case
- **No approved drug and revenue actually missed** — the platform's clinical output is still unproven; "AI-bio" has over-promised industry-wide.
- The "AI" framing invites the **replacement question** — how much is durable biology vs. software that improves everywhere at once.
- History of dilution; crowded field of AI-bio rivals.

**Thesis invalidation:** key pipeline readouts disappoint and partnership milestones stall — i.e., the platform keeps generating data but not clinical wins.

### Path to multiples
- **10x:** multiple pipeline assets read out positively and the platform is validated as a repeatable drug-discovery engine with rich partner economics.
- **100x:** Recursion becomes the dominant "industrialized drug discovery" platform — high payoff, low probability, many clinical gates.
