# RGTI — Rigetti Computing

**Tier: C** · Composite **55.6** · Sector: Quantum computing · Country: USA · Held: Yes (active basket)
Price **$18.71** · Shares 332.4M · **Mkt cap ~$6.22B** · Cash ~$418M · Debt ~$7M · Data Confidence: High · Last verified 2026-06-26

> A full-stack superconducting quantum company that — unusually — **owns its own chip fab (Fab-1)**. Smaller and earlier than IBM/Google/IonQ, but a genuine hardware play.

### Bull case
- **Owns a quantum chip foundry** — a real, hard-to-replicate physical asset and a differentiator versus fabless quantum peers.
- Superconducting qubits are the same family IBM/Google use; Rigetti is the main *investable pure-play* in that modality.
- Strong cash position (~$418M+) and minimal debt give a multi-year runway; DARPA and national-quantum-initiative support.

### Bear case
- **Behind the leaders** on qubit count/fidelity and far behind on resources; risks being out-scaled.
- **Severe dilution** — share count up ~48% in a year; survival has meant selling stock.
- **~$6B cap on negligible revenue** — valuation rides the quantum narrative, not fundamentals.

**Thesis invalidation:** falls decisively behind on error-correction milestones while continuing to dilute — i.e., becomes a perennial also-ran burning shareholder capital.

### Path to multiples
- **10x:** Rigetti hits its superconducting roadmap, error correction progresses, and it becomes a credible #2–3 platform with real bookings.
- **100x:** only in a world where quantum works *and* Rigetti is a winner despite being out-resourced — low probability, high payoff.

**Subjective 5–10yr odds:** P(≥3x) ~30% · P(≥10x) ~10% · P(major permanent loss) ~35%.
