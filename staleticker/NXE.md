### Bull case
- **A world-class orebody** — Arrow's grade and scale could supply a meaningful share of global uranium demand; deposits like this cannot be manufactured.
- **De-risked in 2026:** the CNSC approved the Environmental Assessment and issued the licence to prepare site and construct — a major regulatory milestone.
- Well-funded (C$950M raise + a physical-uranium stockpile) into construction.

### Bear case
- **Pre-revenue** — first production is years out; mine construction carries cost/schedule risk.
- Uranium-price dependent; the equity already prices a lot of future success (~$6.4B for a pre-production miner).
- Dilution from large financing rounds.

**Thesis invalidation:** construction badly overruns or slips, uranium prices roll over, and the company must keep diluting to fund Rook I.

### Path to multiples
- **10x:** Arrow comes online into a structurally tight uranium market and NexGen becomes a top-tier global producer.
- **100x:** not realistic from a $6.4B base.

**Subjective 5–10yr odds:** P(≥3x) ~42% · P(≥10x) ~12% · P(major loss) ~20%.
