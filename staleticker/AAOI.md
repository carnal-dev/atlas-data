> A vertically integrated maker of **optical transceivers** and components, riding the AI-datacenter buildout that's driving insatiable demand for high-speed optical interconnects.

### Bull case
- **AI datacenters need enormous optical bandwidth** — transceivers are a real, growing AI-infrastructure layer, and AAOI's revenue is inflecting (+51%).
- **Vertically integrated** (lasers, components, modules) — owns optical/laser fabs, a genuine hard-asset base versus pure assemblers.
- Hyperscaler design-win momentum and a strengthened balance sheet ($449M cash after a raise).

### Bear case
- **Brutally competitive** transceiver market (Coherent, Lumentum, Innolight) with pricing pressure and share volatility.
- **Historically lumpy and loss-making** — AAOI has boomed and busted before on customer concentration.
- Near the $10B cap with a rich multiple (P/S ~21×) — a lot of AI optimism is priced.

**Thesis invalidation:** hyperscaler demand lumpiness returns or a transition (e.g. silicon photonics / co-packaged optics) leapfrogs AAOI's product line and erodes share.

### Path to multiples
- **10x:** AAOI becomes a durable, profitable optical supplier to multiple hyperscalers as AI bandwidth demand compounds.
- **100x:** unrealistic near a ~$10B cap.

**Subjective 5–10yr odds:** P(≥3x) ~33% · P(≥10x) ~9% · P(major loss) ~25%.
