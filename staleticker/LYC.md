### Bull case
- **The ex-China REE champion** — fully integrated mine-to-separated-oxides, a position that took decades and billions to build.
- **US DoD funding** for the Texas heavy-rare-earth plant; Western governments are actively de-risking the supply chain.
- Profitable, record half-year, expanding heavy-REE (Dy/Tb) capacity — the magnets that matter most for defense/EV.

### Bear case
- **China sets the price** — can compress NdPr margins at will; Lynas is a price-taker on the commodity.
- ~$12B cap means the easy asymmetric money is made; this is now a quality compounder, not an early bet.
- FX and Malaysian regulatory/processing-licence history add noise.

**Thesis invalidation:** China floods the market and crushes NdPr pricing for years while Lynas's heavy-REE ramp disappoints — turning it back into a sub-scale price-taker.

### Path to multiples

**Subjective 5–10yr odds:** P(≥3x) ~32% · P(≥10x) ~6% · P(major loss) ~12%.
