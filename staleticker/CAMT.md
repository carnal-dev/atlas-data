> A maker of **inspection and metrology** systems for semiconductors — the machines that check wafers and packages for defects, especially in the advanced-packaging, HBM, and chiplet steps that AI chips depend on.

### Bull case
- **Advanced packaging is the AI bottleneck**, and inspection/metrology scales with it — Camtek is a leader exactly where HBM and chiplet complexity is exploding.
- **Profitable picks-and-shovels** with ~50% gross margins, net cash, and strong order momentum — quality without single-product binary risk.
- Diversified across IDMs, OSATs, and foundries globally.

### Bear case
- **Semicap is cyclical** — orders swing with the equipment cycle.
- **China exposure** (a meaningful revenue slice) carries geopolitical/export-control risk.
- ~14× sales after a strong run prices in continued AI-packaging demand.

**Thesis invalidation:** an AI-capex digestion cycle plus China export restrictions hit orders, and the advanced-packaging inspection growth stalls.

### Path to multiples
- **10x:** advanced-packaging inspection becomes a structural growth market and Camtek compounds revenue and share into it.
- **100x:** unrealistic for an established profitable semicap.

**Subjective 5–10yr odds:** P(≥3x) ~35% · P(≥10x) ~7% · P(major loss) ~12%.

### Why it scores B (best-in-theme semis)
Competitive Position **70**, Replacement Risk **72**, and a strong **Reality Check (75)** — profitable, real revenue, AI-packaging tailwind. Tops the semis cluster (ahead of KLIC/AEHR/AAOI/NVTS). Cyclicality and China risk keep it B.

**Catalysts:** advanced-packaging / HBM inspection demand · AI-driven order momentum · earnings.
