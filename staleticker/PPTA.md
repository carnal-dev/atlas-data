# PPTA — Perpetua Resources

**Tier: B** · Composite **69.0** · EAR **3.53×** · ★ best-in-theme (Critical minerals) · Country: USA · Held: No
Price **$21.47** · Shares 125M · **Mkt cap ~$2.68B** · Cash ~$669M · Debt ~$0 (+ EXIM ~$2.7B loan proposed) · Pre-revenue (production ~2029) · Data Confidence: High · Last verified 2026-06-26

> Building the Stibnite mine in Idaho — the only near-term domestic source of **antimony**, a defense-critical mineral China has restricted, plus 4.2M oz of gold to fund it.

### Bull case
- **Antimony is a genuine US vulnerability** — essential for munitions, semiconductors, and flame retardants, and China banned exports. Stibnite is the answer.
- **Fully financed and building:** ~$669M cash + a proposed **$2.7B EXIM loan** — the US government is effectively underwriting it.
- The gold co-product (~4.2M oz) provides real cash flow to carry the strategic antimony.

### Bear case
- **First production ~2029** — years of construction, permitting, and execution risk before a dollar of revenue.
- Capital-intensive mine build; cost overruns and schedule slips are the norm in mining.
- Antimony/gold price swings affect the economics.

**Thesis invalidation:** a permitting reversal, EXIM loan falls through, or a major construction/cost blowout pushes first production out and forces dilutive financing.

### Path to multiples
- **10x:** Stibnite comes online as *the* strategic US antimony source with gold cash flow, re-rating on supply-security scarcity.
- **100x:** unrealistic for a single-mine developer.

**Subjective 5–10yr odds:** P(≥3x) ~42% · P(≥10x) ~13% · P(major loss) ~20%.

### Why it scores B (high EAR)
Replacement Risk **92** (a permitted strategic mine is irreplaceable), Civ Importance **85**, Govt Tailwinds **92**. Reality Check **38** (pre-production) caps the tier, but the strong odds + sub-$3B base give it a high **EAR of 3.53×** — among the most asymmetric in the database.

**Catalysts:** EXIM loan close · construction milestones · antimony offtake agreements · first production ~2029.
