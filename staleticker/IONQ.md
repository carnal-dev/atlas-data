**⚠ Cap exception:** >$10B — included as the quantum-leader standout.

> The highest-profile pure-play quantum computer, built on trapped-ion qubits. Aggressively acquiring (Oxford Ionics, Qubitekk) and building toward networked, error-corrected systems.

### Bull case
- **Trapped-ion approach** offers high fidelity and all-to-all connectivity; IonQ leads the pure-play field on mindshare and enterprise partnerships.
- **$2B cash war chest** funds M&A and scaling — buying its way toward photonic interconnects and quantum networking.
- If fault-tolerant quantum arrives, the TAM (chemistry, materials, optimization, cryptography) is civilization-scale.

### Bear case
- **~$19B cap on minimal real revenue** — one of the richest risk/reward setups in the batch; priced for a future that may be a decade away.
- **Technical risk is profound:** error correction at useful scale is unproven for everyone; trapped-ion gate speeds are a knock.
- Growth is partly bought (dilutive M&A), not yet organically commercial.

**Thesis invalidation:** a competing modality (superconducting/neutral-atom) decisively wins the error-correction race, or scaling stalls while cash burns toward the next raise.

### Path to multiples
- **10x from a $19B base is extraordinary** — requires IonQ to become *the* enterprise quantum platform with real, recurring commercial revenue.
- More likely a high-volatility option on quantum working at all; size accordingly.

**Subjective 5–10yr odds (from today's price):** P(≥3x) ~28% · P(≥10x) ~8% · P(major permanent loss) ~30%.
