# MP — MP Materials

**Tier: A** · Composite **75.6** · Sector: Mining / Rare earths · Country: USA · Held: No (new idea)
Price **$54.36** · Shares 178.0M · **Mkt cap ~$9.68B** · Cash $1.74B · Debt $1.05B · Data Confidence: High · Last verified 2026-06-26

> The only scaled rare-earth mine in the Western Hemisphere (Mountain Pass) that is also building the magnet factories downstream. Pure hard-asset, government-backed supply-chain security play.

### Bull case
- **Owns the mine.** Mountain Pass is the only at-scale US rare-earth resource — permitting + geology make it effectively irreplaceable.
- **Moving downstream into magnets** (Independence facility, Fort Worth) — capturing the high-value, China-dominated step. Anchored by **DoD price-floor support and Apple/automaker offtake**.
- Rare earths are foundational to EVs, defense (every F-35 needs them), robotics, and wind — and the US treats this as national security.

### Bear case
- **China controls pricing.** Beijing can flood the market and compress margins at will; NdPr prices have been the swing factor.
- Magnet-manufacturing ramp is execution-heavy and new to the company; capital-intensive with $1.05B debt.
- At ~$9.7B the strategic premium is already partly priced.

**Thesis invalidation:** the magnet-plant ramp stalls/misses for multiple years AND the DoD price-floor / strategic support is withdrawn, leaving MP a price-taking miner again.

### Path to multiples
- **10x:** becomes the vertically integrated "Western magnet champion," selling magnets (not just concentrate) at scale into defense + auto at protected margins.
- **100x:** would require both a sustained rare-earth supercycle and MP capturing most of the ex-China magnet market — possible only over a very long horizon.

**Subjective 5–10yr odds:** P(≥3x) ~40% · P(≥10x) ~10% · P(major permanent loss) ~15%.

Replacement Risk **95** (a mine + magnet plant is the single hardest thing to replace in this batch) and Civilizational Importance **92**. Reality Check **75**: real revenue, real production, real government and customer contracts.

**Catalysts:** magnet-plant ramp · DoD price-floor & Apple deliveries · 10X facility expansion.
