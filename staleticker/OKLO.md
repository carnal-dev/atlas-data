> A fast-reactor SMR developer with a build-own-operate model (sell power, not reactors) and a fuel-recycling ambition. Sam Altman–chaired, exceptionally well-funded, now actually breaking ground.

### Bull case
- **Differentiated model:** Oklo plans to own and operate its Aurora powerhouses and sell electricity under long PPAs — recurring revenue, not one-off reactor sales. Strong fit for **AI-datacenter firm-power demand** (Meta LOI, others).
- **$2.54B cash** after a well-timed ATM — funded through first deployment without near-term distress.
- **Broke ground at INL** under the DOE pilot program; NRC fast-tracked design criteria. Vertical play into fuel recycling adds a second hard-asset moat.

### Bear case
- **No operating reactor and no revenue until ~late 2027** at the earliest — pure execution/regulatory story for now.
- Licensing a novel fast reactor is unprecedented at commercial scale; timelines historically slip.
- ATM dilution funded the war chest; rich valuation prices in a lot of future success.

**Thesis invalidation:** a serious NRC licensing setback, an INL construction failure, or a multi-year slip in first power that breaks the AI-power timing thesis.

### Path to multiples
- **10x:** Aurora reaches commercial operation, the build-own-operate fleet scales against datacenter demand, and fuel recycling becomes a second leg.
- **100x:** Oklo becomes a dominant clean-firm-power utility-of-the-future — huge TAM, but far out and execution-gated.

**Subjective 5–10yr odds:** P(≥3x) ~40% · P(≥10x) ~14% · P(major permanent loss) ~25%.
