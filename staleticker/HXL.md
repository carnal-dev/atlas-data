> One half of the Western carbon-fiber duopoly (with Toray). Its advanced composites are designed into virtually every modern commercial jet and many defense/space platforms.

### Bull case
- **Aerospace-grade carbon fiber is a fortress moat** — qualifications take a decade, are airframe-specific, and almost never get re-competed once designed in.
- **Commercial aero recovery** (Boeing/Airbus build-rate ramp) is a multi-year tailwind, plus growing defense/space content.
- Profitable, disciplined capital allocation (buybacks, deleveraging) — a real industrial compounder.

### Bear case
- **Tied to the aerospace cycle** — build-rate slips at Boeing/Airbus hit directly.
- Customer concentration on two airframers.
- Mature, lower-growth than the speculative names; the upside is compounding, not explosive.

**Thesis invalidation:** a sustained commercial-aero downturn (or a structural shift away from composite-intensive airframes) collapses volumes and pricing.

### Path to multiples
- **A compounder, not a moonshot** — 2–3x over the cycle is the realistic frame; P(≥10x) ~5%.

**Subjective 5–10yr odds:** P(≥3x) ~30% · P(≥10x) ~5% · P(major loss) ~8%.
