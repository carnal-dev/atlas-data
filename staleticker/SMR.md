# SMR — NuScale Power

**Tier: B** · Composite **61.8** · EAR **3.04×** · Theme: Nuclear reactors · Country: USA · Held: No
Price **$10.085** · Shares 346.1M · **Mkt cap ~$3.49B** · Liquidity ~$1B · Debt ~$0 · Pre-commercial · Data Confidence: High · Last verified 2026-06-26

> The first small modular reactor (SMR) developer with an **NRC-certified design** — light-water modules meant to deliver carbon-free, factory-built nuclear power. OKLO's most direct listed comparator.

### Bull case
- **First-mover regulatory moat** — NuScale's design is NRC-certified, a years-and-billions barrier that competitors still face.
- **AI-datacenter power demand** has revived SMR interest after a tough stretch; ~$1B liquidity funds the path to first deployment.
- Modular, factory-built approach could scale faster than conventional reactors if a flagship project lands.

### Bear case
- **A cancelled flagship (UAMPS) haunts it** — NuScale has the scars of a high-profile project failure on cost; credibility must be rebuilt.
- **Pre-commercial with declining revenue** — no operating plant; it needs binding PPAs and equipment orders to inflect.
- Dilution; SMR economics remain unproven at commercial scale.

**Thesis invalidation:** another marquee project cancellation or cost blowout, or failure to convert interest into binding contracts — confirming SMRs aren't yet economic.

### Path to multiples
- **10x:** NuScale lands and delivers a flagship commercial SMR (likely datacenter-anchored), proving the modular model and unlocking a fleet.
- **100x:** the long-shot "SMRs become standard" outcome.

**Subjective 5–10yr odds:** P(≥3x) ~38% · P(≥10x) ~12% · P(major loss) ~25%.
