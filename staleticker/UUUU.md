> The leading US uranium producer *and* an emerging domestic rare-earth processor — it owns the only operating conventional uranium mill in the country (White Mesa, Utah), now also separating rare earths.

### Bull case
- **White Mesa mill is a one-of-a-kind US asset** — licensed, operating, and now producing separated heavy rare earths (first terbium oxide) alongside uranium and vanadium.
- **Two strategic tailwinds at once:** uranium reshoring (Russian-import ban) *and* rare-earth supply-chain security.
- Strong balance sheet (~$910M liquidity, no debt) funds the REE build-out without distress.

### Bear case
- **Uranium-price dependent** for the core business; REE separation is new and unproven at commercial scale for the company.
- ~$70M revenue against a $3.4B cap (P/S ~49×) — a lot of REE optionality is already priced.
- History of dilution (~15%/yr).

**Thesis invalidation:** the rare-earth separation ramp stalls AND uranium prices fade — leaving a richly valued small miner without the REE growth story.

### Path to multiples
- **10x:** White Mesa becomes a meaningful Western source of *separated* heavy rare earths while uranium output scales into a tight market.
- **100x:** unrealistic without becoming a dominant integrated fuel+REE supplier.

**Subjective 5–10yr odds:** P(≥3x) ~42% · P(≥10x) ~12% · P(major loss) ~18%.
