> Maker of the **Hydros** robotic system for **Aquablation** — a heat-free, robotically-controlled water-jet treatment for enlarged prostate (BPH) that's taking share in a large urology market.

### Bull case
- **Differentiated, FDA-cleared robotic platform** with clinical evidence and growing surgeon adoption (~12,200 US procedures/quarter) — real switching costs once a hospital standardizes on it.
- **Razor-and-blade economics:** place systems, then earn recurring per-procedure handpiece revenue as utilization compounds.
- **Cheap for the category** — P/S ~3× on 20%+ growth, a steep derating from its premium-medtech peak.

### Bear case
- **Still loss-making** as it invests in the commercial build-out.
- **Single-platform/single-indication** today — concentrated on BPH; expansion (new indications, ex-US) is unproven.
- Competes with established BPH therapies and the broader surgical-robotics field.

**Thesis invalidation:** procedure growth and system placements decelerate, or reimbursement/clinical setbacks stall adoption — undercutting the recurring-revenue flywheel.

### Path to multiples
- **10x:** Aquablation becomes a standard of care in BPH and expands into new indications/geographies, with the installed base driving high-margin recurring revenue.
- **100x:** unlikely without becoming a multi-indication surgical-robotics platform.

**Subjective 5–10yr odds:** P(≥3x) ~42% · P(≥10x) ~10% · P(major loss) ~15%.
