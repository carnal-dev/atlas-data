> The leader in semiconductor **wire bonding** equipment, now pushing into **advanced packaging** — thermo-compression bonding (TCB) and vertical-wire tools used to assemble AI accelerators and HBM stacks.

### Bull case
- **Advanced packaging is where Moore's Law continues** — chiplets and HBM make assembly/packaging a strategic bottleneck, and KLIC's TCB tools target it directly (~$400M TCB sales goal by FY27).
- **Picks-and-shovels for AI** without single-customer binary risk; profitable with a clean, net-cash balance sheet.
- Entrenched installed base in bonding gives distribution and service leverage into the new tools.

### Bear case
- **Cyclical semicap** — revenue swings with the equipment cycle; the core wire-bonding business is mature.
- TCB is **competitive** (BESI, ASMPT) and KLIC is a challenger there, not the incumbent.
- Insider selling was flagged into the 2026 strength.

**Thesis invalidation:** TCB design-ins go to BESI/ASMPT and the core bonding business stays cyclical/flat — leaving KLIC without the advanced-packaging growth leg.

### Path to multiples
- **10x:** TCB becomes a major standard in AI/HBM assembly and KLIC captures a real share, transforming the growth profile.
- **100x:** unrealistic for an established semicap.

**Subjective 5–10yr odds:** P(≥3x) ~33% · P(≥10x) ~7% · P(major loss) ~15%.

Replacement Risk **70** (semicap IP + installed base), Reality Check **68** (profitable, real revenue, TCB traction), Civ Importance **72**. Cyclicality and challenger-status in TCB hold it mid-B. Best-in-theme of the semis cluster (ahead of AEHR/NVTS/AAOI).
