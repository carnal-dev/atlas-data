Price **$17.26** · Shares 230.8M · **Mkt cap ~$3.98B** · Cash $223M · Debt ~$0 · Revenue TTM ~$46M (declining) · P/S ~87× · Data Confidence: High · Last verified 2026-06-26

> A **GaN and SiC power-semiconductor** designer — next-gen power electronics for fast chargers, EVs, solar, and increasingly AI-datacenter power. Fabless, fast-story, very richly valued.

### Bull case
- **GaN/SiC are the future of power conversion** — higher efficiency and density than silicon, with a large addressable market in EVs, datacenters, and renewables.
- A pivot toward **high-power** (datacenter, EV) products could re-accelerate growth into a structural trend.
- Clean balance sheet (no debt, $223M cash) buys time for the inflection.

### Bear case
- **Revenue is actually declining** (−39% YoY in Q1) — the growth story is on hold, yet the stock trades at **~87× sales**.
- **Fabless** (relies on TSMC) — limited hard-asset moat; competes with Infineon, onsemi, Power Integrations, who have scale and fabs.
- The valuation leaves enormous room to de-rate if design wins don't convert.

**Thesis invalidation:** high-power design wins fail to convert to revenue while the broader power-semi market stays soft — and the ~87× multiple compresses hard.

### Path to multiples
- **10x:** GaN/SiC adoption inflects, Navitas lands major datacenter/EV sockets, and revenue compounds into the valuation.
- **100x:** unrealistic from a ~$4B cap on declining revenue.

**Subjective 5–10yr odds:** P(≥3x) ~30% · P(≥10x) ~10% · P(major loss) ~30%.

### Why it scores C
Decent TAM (**75**) and Civ Importance (**70**), but **Manufacturing 25** (fabless), **Valuation 20** (~87× sales), and **Reality Check 38** (declining revenue) pull the Composite to 48.9. The most valuation-stretched name in Batch 2 — a high-variance bet on an inflection that hasn't arrived.

**Catalysts:** high-power GaN/SiC design-win conversion · AI-datacenter & EV power ramps.
