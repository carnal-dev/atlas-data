### Bull case
- **Only Western company besides SpaceX flying orbital rockets at cadence.** Vertical integration (engines, structures, avionics, launch sites) is a deep manufacturing moat.
- **Neutron** opens the medium-lift / constellation-deployment market and national-security launch — a step-change in TAM.
- Space Systems (satellites, components, Flatellite) already generates the majority of revenue and diversifies beyond launch.

### Bear case
- **Valuation is extreme** (~$54B vs. sub-$1B revenue) — the highest in this batch; priced for flawless execution.
- **Neutron is the whole bull case and it hasn't flown.** Slips/failures would hit hard.
- Still unprofitable; space is unforgiving operationally.

**Thesis invalidation:** repeated Neutron failures or multi-year slips, or an Electron reliability problem that breaks the cadence story.

### Path to multiples
- **10x from here is very hard** at a $54B base — would need Neutron to become a workhorse plus Space Systems scaling into a prime-contractor-sized franchise.
- More realistically a **compounder**: the asymmetric entry was years ago; today it's "own quality, expect volatility."
