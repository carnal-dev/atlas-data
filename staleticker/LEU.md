# LEU — Centrus Energy

**Tier: A** · Composite **76.9** · Sector: Nuclear (enrichment / HALEU) · Country: USA · Held: No (new idea)
Price **$165.74** · Shares 19.67M · **Mkt cap ~$3.26B** · Cash $1.87B · Debt $1.21B · Data Confidence: High · Last verified 2026-06-26

> The only US-owned uranium enrichment company — and the only domestic source of HALEU, the fuel almost every advanced reactor and SMR design needs. Profitable, contracted, and strategically irreplaceable.

### Bull case
- **De-facto US enrichment monopoly** with an NRC license and operating centrifuge cascade at Piketon, Ohio — a near-impossible asset to replicate (decades, billions, security clearances).
- **HALEU is the choke point** for the entire advanced-reactor wave (Oklo, X-energy, TerraPower). $900M DOE task order for commercial-scale HALEU plus funded option years.
- Russian-uranium import ban + reshoring politics = structural, bipartisan tailwind. **Already profitable** (Q1 2026 net profit).

### Bear case
- Capital intensity is enormous; scaling enrichment capacity needs sustained government co-funding.
- Tiny float (19.7M shares) → violent volatility (already ran ~385%); valuation now rich vs. current LEU-segment revenue.
- Execution/timeline risk on capacity expansion; some revenue still tied to a legacy SWU resale book.

**Thesis invalidation:** DOE walks away from / fails to finalize the commercial HALEU agreements, or a competing enrichment supply (Urenco/Orano US expansion) erases the domestic-monopoly premium.

### Path to multiples
- **10x:** HALEU demand materializes on schedule, Centrus becomes the contracted backbone supplier at scale, margins expand as fixed enrichment costs leverage.
- **100x:** unlikely from a ~$3B base without a generational nuclear buildout; more a durable compounder than a moonshot.

**Subjective 5–10yr odds:** P(≥3x) ~45% · P(≥10x) ~12% · P(major permanent loss) ~15%.

### Why it scores high
Replacement Risk **93** and Reality Check **80** — real revenue, real profit, real DOE contracts, and an asset base (centrifuges + license) that is the antithesis of a software moat. Civilizational Importance **88**: nuclear fuel underpins clean firm power.

**Catalysts:** $900M HALEU definitive agreement · enrichment capacity expansion · DOE option-year funding.
