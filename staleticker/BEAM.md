> The leader in **base editing** — a more precise next generation of gene editing that rewrites single DNA letters without cutting the double helix, potentially safer than first-gen CRISPR.

### Bull case
- **Base editing is a genuine technical leap** — single-base precision avoids the double-strand breaks that worry first-gen CRISPR; deep IP moat.
- **Funded into mid-2029** ($1.2B) — covers the launch of risto-cel (sickle cell), the BEAM-302 pivotal (AATD), and BEAM-304 proof-of-concept (PKU).
- A platform that could generalize across many genetic diseases if the lead programs validate.

### Bear case
- **Pre-commercial and binary** — value hinges on clinical readouts; no approved product yet (unlike CRSP).
- Base editing is newer/less clinically proven than conventional CRISPR — more unknowns.
- Dilution is a recurring feature of pre-revenue biotech.

**Thesis invalidation:** a pivotal failure or safety signal in risto-cel or BEAM-302, or an FDA setback that breaks the late-stage narrative.

### Path to multiples
- **10x:** risto-cel and BEAM-302 reach approval and base editing is validated as a safer, broadly applicable platform.
- **100x:** the long-shot "programmable cures" outcome across many indications.

**Subjective 5–10yr odds:** P(≥3x) ~40% · P(≥10x) ~14% · P(major loss) ~30%.
